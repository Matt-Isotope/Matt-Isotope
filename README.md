## Hi there 👋

# Matt Isotope

Researcher specialised in stable isotope analysis, with a particular focus on non-traditional isotopes applied to the study of archaeological human remains.

I earned my PhD at Sapienza University of Rome, where I developed interdisciplinary research at the intersection of archaeology, bioarchaeology, geochemistry, and scientific data analysis.

## Research interests
- Stable isotope analysis
- Non-traditional isotopes
- Archaeological human remains
- Archaeology
- Geochemistry
- Scientific data analysis
- AI-assisted research workflows
- Art-Science interaction
- Nondisciplinarity
- Hybrid arts

## Projects

## Outreach and public engagement
I am a co-creator of the interdisciplinary Third Mission project “SAGacia”, which explores the intrinsic connection between art and science, alongside other outreach initiatives designed to make research more accessible and engaging to wider audiences.
